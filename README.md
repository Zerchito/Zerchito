## How I got here

I originally wanted to be a high school history or physics teacher. When it was time to choose a degree, I was split between systems engineering and industrial engineering, and I settled it with a coin flip.

Systems won, and I'm glad it did. I found my passion through **competitive programming at the ICPC** and through teaching as a university teaching assistant. Today I build software for a living and still get to teach.

## What I do

For the last 8 years I've been a full-stack engineer at **Witco**, a Paris-based workplace and tenant-experience platform. My recent work includes:

- **Modernizing legacy code:** migrating core features from Meteor to NestJS while keeping web and mobile clients working without changes
- **API security:** hardening our GraphQL API after a security audit
- **Cloud services:** owning a Node.js microservice on AWS ECS, with infrastructure in Terraform
- **Integrations:** connecting the platform with Microsoft 365, ticketing, access-control and HR systems

I'm known for staying calm with complex problems. Debugging is my favorite part of the job: every bug is a puzzle worth understanding.

## Teaching

I teach **Introduction to Programming** at Jala University. Helping someone write their first working program is one of the most rewarding parts of my week.
